---

The system does the routine work and stops whenever a number looks wrong. A person sees the problem before anything is filed or sent. I would build bookkeeping first, because payroll and tax both depend on clean books.

## What runs, and what you still do

| Stage | Tools | What you still do by hand |
|---|---|---|
| [Bookkeeping](/leafs-loonies-automation-plan/bookkeeping/) | QuickBooks Online, Claude, Python, n8n, Google Workspace | Answer flagged questions, sign off each month |
| [Payroll](/leafs-loonies-automation-plan/payroll/) | Wagepoint, QuickBooks Online, Python, n8n | Approve and run each payroll |
| [Tax prep](/leafs-loonies-automation-plan/tax/) | QuickBooks Online Accountant, your CRA-certified tax software, Python, Claude | Review, get the client's authorization, file |

I have used n8n, not Make or Zapier. If your tools already run on one of those, I would learn it.

## How I would roll it out

Nothing touches a real client until it has run on a fake client's books with errors planted in them, and caught them.

| Probation | Bookkeeping | Payroll | Tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Runs beside your current process on one client, compared line by line | Answer the Wagepoint questions | Not started |
| Month 2 | Live on that client, then more | Runs beside your current process | Not started |
| Month 3 | Normal work | Live with your approval | GST/HST live for one client, T2 tested on a past year |

This is a target, and I would not move a stage forward until the numbers support it. You can judge me on three figures for each client: minutes of human time per month, share of lines that needed no person, and wrong numbers caught versus missed.
